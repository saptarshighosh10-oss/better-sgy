## Detailed description
Better SGY gives your school's Schoology a calmer, faster, student-first redesign —
without changing your grades, your account, or how Schoology works underneath.

It sits on top of your existing Schoology site and reorganizes everything you actually
use into one clean dashboard:

• Overview — a tidy snapshot of every class at a glance
• Grades — readable grade breakdowns plus your grade history over time
• Assignments — everything due, sorted and out of your way
• Materials — browse course folders, open files, and even take quizzes inline
• Calendar — your schedule, decluttered
• Announcements — teacher messages, notifications, and class updates in one inbox,
  with a badge that pings only until you've read them
• Nostalgia — a timeline of your school years
• Arcade — a little break room of built-in mini-games

Make it yours with light/dark themes and accent colors, and flip back to the original
Schoology at any time with one click.

Private by design: Better SGY reads your Schoology pages using your own logged-in
session and keeps everything on your device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no
tracking — none of your data ever leaves your browser. (See the privacy policy.)

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Schoology or PowerSchool. "Schoology"
is a trademark of its respective owner and is used here only to describe compatibility.

## Single purpose (for the review form)
Better SGY restyles and reorganizes a logged-in user's own Schoology pages into a
cleaner student dashboard. That is its single purpose.

## Permission justifications (for the review form)
- host_permission `https://*.schoology.com/*`: The extension's entire function is to
  read and re-render the user's own Schoology pages. It requests no other sites.
- `storage`: Saves the user's preferences, theme, and dashboard/grade snapshots locally
  so they persist between page loads. No data is transmitted anywhere.
- Remote code: None. All code (including three.js) is bundled in the package; nothing is
  loaded from a remote server.
